# Threvelonbase SEO, Credibility, and Content Audit

**Audit target:** `audit/seo-credibility` at starting commit `53b8e8cb20120a8e7e3710ee5371dd870221d5d3`

**Audit date:** 2026-08-11

**Scope:** Read-only inspection of the full tracked repository, including the App Router source, rendered copy, metadata, public assets, tests, package/configuration files, build and Worker deployment files, and all repository history available on the branch.

## Evidence Boundary

This report uses repository evidence only. It does not verify the live website, Google Business Profile, Google Maps result, Search Console, Analytics, social profiles, phone numbers, email address, opening hours, premises, business registration, staff, credentials, image provenance, or customer outcomes.

Statements below such as the address, service availability, founding year, and operating hours are described as **claims presented by the source**, not as independently verified facts. The Git history shows when a claim was added to this codebase; it does not prove the underlying business fact.

## Executive Summary

The page has a useful local SEO foundation: the brand, Akure, Ondo State, Arakale Road, repair intent, devices, training, telephone contact, WhatsApp contact, and opening hours are visible in crawlable page copy. The document structure also has one clear H1, ordered H2/H3 sections, a labelled navigation, a footer, and descriptive hero-image alt text.

The site is not ready to be treated as a credible local business result without owner verification and technical SEO completion. The most important gaps are:

1. A development marker is deliberately emitted in page metadata: `codex-preview=development` (`app/layout.tsx:10-12`), and the only rendered-HTML test asserts it (`tests/rendered-html.test.mjs:4-32`). This must not reach a production indexable site.
2. There is no repository `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `app/robots.ts`, `app/sitemap.ts`, canonical URL, or declared public origin.
3. There is no `application/ld+json` or other LocalBusiness structured data.
4. Open Graph and Twitter/X sharing metadata are absent, so shared links have no controlled social title, description, or image.
5. Most credibility signals are unsubstantiated marketing assertions. There are no reviews, testimonials, named technicians, credentials, certifications, registration details, warranties, before/after evidence, case studies, or provenance record for the hero image.
6. The local contact surface is visible but not semantically or operationally complete: the address is a generic span, hours are plain text, a second phone number is displayed but not linked, and the Google Maps link is a generic query rather than a verified place URL.

Overall assessment: **local relevance is promising; technical local SEO readiness is low; credibility readiness is low until claims and evidence are verified.**

## Findings

### 1. Production metadata exposes a development preview marker

**Severity:** Critical

**Evidence:** `app/layout.tsx:6-16` emits `other: { "codex-preview": "development" }`. `tests/rendered-html.test.mjs:4-5` defines a regex for that marker and `tests/rendered-html.test.mjs:7-32` requires it in the response.

**Impact:** A production page can visibly identify itself as a development preview in HTML metadata. This weakens trust and can create uncertainty about whether the business page is final. The test currently protects the preview state rather than production SEO requirements.

**Recommendation:** Establish an explicit production metadata contract before launch. Remove the preview marker from production output, or ensure it is injected only in a non-indexable preview environment. Replace the test assertion with tests for the intended production title, description, canonical, social metadata, and indexation controls.

### 2. Crawl discovery and canonicalization are not implemented in the repository

**Severity:** High

**Evidence:** Tracked files contain no `public/robots.txt`, `public/sitemap.xml`, `app/robots.ts`, or `app/sitemap.ts`. `app/layout.tsx:6-16` has no `metadataBase` or `alternates.canonical`. `next.config.ts` is empty apart from the default config object (`next.config.ts:1-7`). `.openai/hosting.json:1-5` contains a project ID, but no public site origin.

**Impact:** Search engines have no repository-defined sitemap or robots policy and no explicit preferred URL. The audit cannot confirm whether the deployed hostname, HTTP-to-HTTPS behavior, trailing-slash behavior, or duplicate-host handling are correct.

**Recommendation:** Confirm the production hostname first. Define a self-referencing canonical for the homepage, add a minimal sitemap containing only the canonical indexable URL, add a robots policy that allows the public page and references that sitemap, and verify the deployed responses. Do not publish a guessed domain or sitemap URL.

### 3. LocalBusiness structured data is absent

**Severity:** High

**Evidence:** No tracked file contains `application/ld+json`, `LocalBusiness`, `Organization`, `PostalAddress`, `OpeningHoursSpecification`, or `sameAs`. The visible business details are ordinary JSX text and links (`app/page.tsx:354-366`).

**Impact:** Search engines receive no machine-readable identity, address, telephone, or hours entity from the repository. This reduces local business disambiguation and makes consistency harder to validate. Structured data is not a guarantee of enhanced results, but its absence is a clear implementation gap.

**Recommendation:** After verifying the real business category and details, add one accurate JSON-LD entity using the narrowest appropriate LocalBusiness subtype. Include only verified `name`, `url`, `telephone`, `address`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `image`, `logo`, and official `sameAs` values. Add `geo`, `priceRange`, `areaServed`, `aggregateRating`, or review properties only when the underlying values are documented and eligible. Validate the rendered output with a schema validator and Google Rich Results Test.

### 4. Social sharing metadata is absent

**Severity:** Medium

**Evidence:** `app/layout.tsx:6-16` defines only `title`, `description`, a preview marker, and icons. There are no Open Graph or Twitter/X metadata fields, no `metadataBase`, and no declared social image. The only social destination in the page is an Instagram link (`app/page.tsx:370-376`).

**Impact:** Link previews may fall back to uncontrolled or incomplete data. The page has no defined share image, locale, site name, card type, or absolute URL.

**Recommendation:** Once the production origin and an approved brand image are known, add absolute Open Graph and Twitter/X metadata. Use a real, licensed brand asset or approved photograph, descriptive alternative text where supported, and dimensions that match the generated file. Do not use the hero image as a real workshop photograph unless its provenance and depiction are confirmed.

### 5. Local relevance is present but shallow and single-page

**Severity:** Medium

**Evidence:** Akure appears in the title and description (`app/layout.tsx:7-9`), the hero lead (`app/page.tsx:160-163`), the trust row (`app/page.tsx:170-172`), the About copy (`app/page.tsx:345-350`), and the address/map query (`app/page.tsx:362`). Arakale Road and Ondo State appear only in the contact block (`app/page.tsx:362`).

**Impact:** The homepage is aligned with searches such as electronics repair in Akure, but it provides little independently useful local detail beyond the business claim and contact block. There are no service-specific pages, service-area explanation, local FAQs, directions, parking/access information, or locally grounded first-hand content. A single long page also limits distinct search-intent coverage.

**Recommendation:** Keep Akure and verified surrounding-location references natural and consistent. Add useful service detail, directions, FAQs, and genuinely local first-hand information only where the business can support it. Create separate pages only for real services or locations with materially unique content; do not create thin city or service doorway pages.

### 6. Address, telephone, and hours need verification and semantic markup

**Severity:** High

**Evidence:** The source presents `Shop 12A, Cash Hold Shopping Complex, Arakale Road, Akure, Ondo State` in `app/page.tsx:362`. It presents `+234 803 772 2368` and `+234 903 608 8295` in `app/page.tsx:363`, but `PHONE_NUMBER` is only the first number (`app/page.tsx:30-31`) and the second number is plain text. Hours appear in both `app/page.tsx:123-125` and `app/page.tsx:364` as `Monday-Saturday, 8:00 AM-6:00 PM`.

**Impact:** NAP and hours are important local trust signals, but the repository cannot prove they are current. The page gives no holiday-hours behavior or timezone. The second phone cannot be clicked or used by the repair flow. The address is not wrapped in `<address>`, and hours are not represented with `time` elements or structured data.

**Recommendation:** Obtain a single owner-approved NAP and hours record, including holiday exceptions and the preferred WhatsApp number. Standardize the display, make every approved phone number actionable, use semantic `<address>` and `<time>` markup, and reuse the same verified values in JSON-LD. Do not normalize, shorten, geocode, or embellish the address without confirmation.

### 7. Google Maps is a generic search link, not verified place integration

**Severity:** High

**Evidence:** `app/page.tsx:362` links to `https://maps.google.com/?q=Shop+12A+Cash+Hold+Shopping+Complex+Arakale+Road+Akure`.

**Impact:** A free-text query can resolve to the wrong place, multiple results, or no durable business listing. It does not establish a Google Business Profile relationship, verified coordinates, Place ID, hours, reviews, or directions for the business.

**Recommendation:** Verify the actual Google Business Profile and use its official share URL or a verified Place ID/map destination. Test the destination on mobile and desktop, confirm the pin and displayed NAP match the owner-approved record, and do not invent coordinates or a listing URL.

### 8. Credibility copy is assertion-heavy and lacks authentic evidence

**Severity:** High

**Evidence:** The page uses claims and trust language including `Expert repairs` (`app/page.tsx:160`), `Professional` and `quality` (`app/page.tsx:162`), `Since 2020` and `Hands-on repair experience` (`app/page.tsx:170`), `Precision diagnostics` and `Hardware + software expertise` (`app/page.tsx:184-185`), `One trusted place` (`app/page.tsx:193`), `Real workshop experience` (`app/page.tsx:319-322`), `usually the fastest way` (`app/page.tsx:359-366`), and service status labels such as `Active`, `Active & developing`, `Emerging`, and `Planned` (`app/page.tsx:335-341`).

**Impact:** These statements may be accurate, but the repository supplies no corroboration. Search users cannot assess who performs the work, what training or qualifications exist, what outcomes are typical, what is covered by a repair, or whether the premises and services are currently available. Unqualified superlatives and trust language can reduce perceived authenticity.

**Recommendation:** Replace broad assertions with specific, owner-approved facts and evidence. Add named people only with consent, real credentials only with documentation, actual repair examples only with permission, clear warranty/diagnosis limits only if offered, and authentic review content only with customer permission and a source. Keep emerging/planned labels accurate and time-bound. Do not add star ratings or review markup without a verifiable review source.

### 9. The hero image has no provenance record and is too heavy for an above-fold raw image

**Severity:** Medium

**Evidence:** `public/images/threvelonbase-repair-hero.png` is a 1,448 x 1,086 RGB PNG, 2,186,538 bytes in the first storefront commit. It is rendered with a raw `<img>` at `app/page.tsx:175-183`, with width, height, `fetchPriority="high"`, and alt text saying `An electronics technician carrying out a precision smartphone repair` (`app/page.tsx:177-183`). No source, photographer, license, consent, capture date, or workshop-identification record is tracked.

**Impact:** A roughly 2.1 MB PNG is a likely LCP cost on mobile, and the raw image has no responsive source set or modern-format alternative. More importantly, users may read the image and alt text as proof of an actual Threvelonbase technician or workshop when the repository does not establish that.

**Recommendation:** Confirm whether the image is an owned, licensed, consented, real business photograph or an illustrative asset. Record provenance outside the public page and use a truthful caption/alt text. Optimize a real approved asset into responsive WebP/AVIF variants while preserving width/height to prevent layout shift. Do not label a stock, generated, or unrelated image as an actual team member or repair performed by this business.

### 10. Trust and compliance information is incomplete

**Severity:** Medium

**Evidence:** The page collects name and phone number (`app/page.tsx:262-281`) and opens a prefilled WhatsApp message in a new window (`app/page.tsx:104-117`). It says no online payment is required and the user reviews the message (`app/page.tsx:257-283`). The footer provides a Gmail address and Instagram destination (`app/page.tsx:370-376`), but there are no privacy, terms, repair warranty, returns, data-handling, or business-identity links in the tracked files.

**Impact:** Prospective customers are asked to provide personal details without a visible privacy explanation or clear statement of how WhatsApp handles the message. Missing terms and repair limitations leave important expectations unclear. This is primarily a trust/conversion risk, with potential legal/compliance implications depending on the operator and jurisdiction.

**Recommendation:** Obtain appropriate owner/legal guidance and publish only policies the business actually follows. Explain what the form does, which data is sent to WhatsApp, how long it is retained, and how to contact the operator about data. Add repair diagnosis, parts, warranty, payment, returns, and appointment terms only after they are approved and accurate.

### 11. Internal linking is coherent but does not support deeper crawlable content

**Severity:** Low to Medium

**Evidence:** Navigation and footer links point to same-page anchors (`app/page.tsx:33-39`, `app/page.tsx:135-142`, `app/page.tsx:370-374`). Service cards point to anchors or WhatsApp (`app/page.tsx:52-87`). There are no links to dedicated service, location, policy, review, or educational pages.

**Impact:** The page is easy to scan and the anchor structure is coherent, but search engines and users have no deeper first-party content to evaluate. The `Learn more` labels on cards do not lead to separate explanations for most services.

**Recommendation:** Keep the current compact architecture if the site is intentionally one page, but make anchor labels and section content satisfy the corresponding intent. Add deeper pages only when they contain genuinely unique, useful, and verified information. Link to approved policies and official profiles from relevant areas.

### 12. SEO and credibility behavior is not covered by tests

**Severity:** Medium

**Evidence:** `tests/rendered-html.test.mjs:7-32` checks only a 200 response, HTML content type, and the development preview meta tag. There are no tests for H1 count, title/description, canonical, robots, sitemap, Open Graph/Twitter tags, JSON-LD, NAP consistency, hours, or external-link destinations.

**Impact:** SEO regressions can ship while the only test continues to pass. The current test would also pass if important production metadata were absent, provided the development marker remained.

**Recommendation:** Add rendered-output assertions after the production metadata contract is decided. Cover one H1, required title and description, canonical origin, indexation policy, social tags, valid JSON-LD, exact owner-approved NAP/hours, and the verified Maps destination. Add a small link/asset smoke check for public paths.

### 13. Deployment configuration does not establish public SEO behavior

**Severity:** Medium

**Evidence:** `vite.config.ts:36-63` configures Vinext, Cloudflare, and local bindings. `worker/index.ts:28-43` routes image optimization and delegates other requests. `build/sites-vite-plugin.ts:27-43` copies only the hosting manifest and Drizzle directory into `dist/.openai`. `scripts/build-verified.sh:10-28` and `scripts/install-ci.sh:10-25` require GNU/Linux tooling such as `timeout`, `flock`, `curl`, and `sha256sum`; `README.md:14-20` documents the remote build assumptions. No CI workflow, live-host validation, response-header test, or deployment smoke test is tracked.

**Impact:** A successful source build would not prove that the deployed hostname, HTML metadata, static SEO files, HTTPS redirects, cache headers, image responses, or preview protection are correct. Local macOS audit execution is additionally constrained by the documented Linux-only lifecycle scripts.

**Recommendation:** Add deployment smoke checks in the real build environment. Verify `/`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, favicon, hero variants, status codes, content types, canonical origin, cache/security headers, and production preview/indexation behavior against the actual hostname. Keep preview environments protected from indexing.

## Metadata and Semantic Structure Review

### Present

- The root document declares `lang="en"` (`app/layout.tsx:19-28`).
- The page has one visible H1, `Expert repairs for phones, laptops & everyday electronics` (`app/page.tsx:156-163`).
- Sections use descriptive H2s and service/process/business H3s (`app/page.tsx:190-352`).
- The page uses `main`, `header`, `nav`, `section`, `article`, and `footer` elements.
- The primary navigation has `aria-label="Primary navigation"`; the mobile menu has an accessible label and `aria-expanded` state (`app/page.tsx:135-152`).
- The hero image has explicit dimensions, which helps reserve layout space (`app/page.tsx:177-183`).
- The address, phones, hours, email, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Maps pathways are visible in page content.

### Missing or weak

- No explicit canonical, robots policy, sitemap, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, `metadataBase`, manifest, or JSON-LD.
- Contact details are not marked up as `<address>`; hours are not marked up with `<time>` or a machine-readable hours specification.
- The logo images are intentionally empty-alt decorative images beside a text brand name, which is acceptable for the visible wordmark but means social/structured metadata needs a separate approved image reference.
- The form has labels but no `name` attributes, no server submission, no privacy link, and no consent or data-use explanation. This is not a direct ranking issue, but it is a credibility and operational gap.
- No author, reviewer, owner, technician, or editorial update information exists for factual or training content.

## LocalBusiness Data Checklist

Before implementing structured data or local profile changes, obtain an owner-approved source of truth for:

- Legal/trading name and the business category actually used by customers.
- Complete address, suite/shop identifier, access instructions, and verified map pin.
- Primary and secondary phone numbers, WhatsApp number, and preferred contact route.
- Regular hours, timezone, holiday exceptions, and whether the workshop is appointment-only.
- Official website origin, verified Google Business Profile URL, and official social profile URLs.
- Owned/licensed logo and real business photographs.
- Any credentials, registrations, warranties, service guarantees, review sources, or training outcomes the business is willing to publish.

Do not add `aggregateRating`, `review`, `priceRange`, `geo`, `areaServed`, `sameAs`, employee credentials, certifications, or performance statistics from assumptions. Do not claim Google verification merely because a Maps query link exists.

3. Review hours, availability, contact routing, Google profile data, social links, and evidence freshness on a defined cadence.
